# Computational Design & Fabrication: A Reading Roadmap

## Roadmap Progression Levels

* **[Level 1] Foundations & Parametric Thinking:** Introductory theory, historical context, and the basics of visual scripting and digital fabrication.
* **[Level 2] Algorithmic Logic & Data Structures:** Intermediate texts bridging the gap from visual canvases to understanding underlying mathematical logic, data trees, and material behaviors.
* **[Level 3] Advanced Research & Custom Tooling:** Bleeding-edge fabrication methods, computational geometry, and peer-reviewed proceedings for students ready to develop novel workflows or custom scripts.
