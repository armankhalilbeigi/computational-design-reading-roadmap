# Computational Design & Fabrication: A Reading Roadmap

## Reading Guide & Markers

* **`[★]` Essentials (Start Here):** Foundational theory, historical context, and the entry points for visual scripting and digital fabrication.
* **Unmarked — Core Curriculum:** Intermediate texts, manuals, and methodologies bridging visual canvases, algorithmic logic, and material behavior.
* **`[◆]` Advanced Research:** Specialized computational geometry, custom tooling, and peer-reviewed research proceedings.

## 3. Key Conferences & Journals (Categorized by Domain)

### • Digital Fabrication & Robotics

* **ACADIA** (Association for Computer Aided Design in Architecture) — _Conference_
* **Rob|Arch** (Robotic Fabrication in Architecture, Art, and Design) — _Conference_
* **3D Printing and Additive Manufacturing** — _Journal_
* **Automation in Construction** — _Journal_

### • Computational Geometry & Mathematics

* **AAG** (Advances in Architectural Geometry) — _Conference_
* **Nexus Journal** (Architecture and Mathematics) — _Journal_

### • General Computer-Aided Architectural Design (CAAD)

* **eCAADe** (Education and Research in Computer Aided Architectural Design in Europe) — _Conference_
* **CAADRIA** (Association for Computer Aided Architectural Design Research in Asia) — _Conference_
* **SIGRADI** (Iberoamerican Society of Digital Graphics) — _Conference_
* **CAAD Futures** — _Conference_
* **IJAC** (International Journal of Architectural Computing) — _Journal_

### • Design Theory, AI, & Cognition

* **DCC** (Design Computing and Cognition) — _Conference_
* **AIEDAM** (Artificial Intelligence for Engineering Design, Analysis, and Manufacturing) — _Journal_
* **Design Science** — _Journal_
* **Design Studies** — _Journal_

### • Computer Graphics, Vision, & Urban Systems

* **SIGGRAPH** (ACM Special Interest Group on Computer Graphics and Interactive Techniques) — _Conference_
* **Environment and Planning B: Planning and Design** — _Journal_

### • Building Simulation & Environmental Performance:

* **SimAUD** (Symposium on Simulation for Architecture and Urban Design) — _Conference_ (The absolute premier venue for coupling computational design workflows with performance simulation).
* **ANNSIM** (Annual Modeling and Simulation Conference) — _Conference_ (Highly technical; often hosts built-environment simulation tracks).
* **IBPSA Building Simulation** (International Building Performance Simulation Association) — _Conference/Journal_
* **CISBAT** (International Conference on Cleantech for Smart Cities & Buildings) — _Conference_ (Great for students looking at energy modeling and sustainable technologies).
